💜 Need support?

If someone is being bullied, you deserve to be heard and supported.

  • Talk to a trusted adult, school counselor, teacher, or parent.
  • If you're in immediate danger, get help from a nearby adult or emergency services.
  • Save evidence of online bullying and use the platform's report/block tools.
  • Use your school's official reporting process.

This app is not an emergency or anonymous reporting service. It does not collect personal bullying stories.
